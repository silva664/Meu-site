The 10 Recipes

Each recipe serves 2 to 4 people unless noted. Use measuring cups and a food scale for the most consistent results.

Recipe 01

Greek Yogurt Berry Parfait

Serves 15 minBreakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (nonfat or 2%)
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries, fresh or frozen
  • 1/4 cup low-sugar granola
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)
  • 1 tbsp chopped walnuts

Steps

  1. Spoon half the yogurt into a glass or jar.
  2. Add half the berries and half the granola.
  3. Repeat the layers, then top with walnuts and a drizzle of honey.
Recipe 02

Lemon Herb Chicken Salad

Serves 225 minLunch

Ingredients

  • 8 oz boneless chicken breast
  • 1 lemon (juice and zest)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, salt, and pepper
  • 5 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced

Steps

  1. Rub the chicken with lemon zest, oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat, 5 to 6 minutes per side, until the internal temperature reaches 165 F (74 C). Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
  3. Toss the greens, tomatoes, and cucumber with lemon juice and a pinch of salt. Top with the chicken.
Recipe 03

Veggie Egg White Scramble

Serves 110 minBreakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1/2 cup baby spinach
  • 1/4 cup diced bell pepper
  • 2 tbsp diced onion
  • 2 tbsp shredded part-skim mozzarella
  • Salt, pepper, and smoked paprika

Steps

  1. Saute the onion and pepper in a nonstick pan with a spray of oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the spinach and cook until wilted.
  3. Pour in the egg whites, season, and gently stir until just set. Sprinkle with cheese and serve.
Recipe 04

Turkey Lettuce Wraps

Serves 320 minLunch

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey (93% lean)
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar and 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 small carrot, shredded
  • 8 butter lettuce leaves

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey in the sesame oil over medium heat, breaking it apart, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and ginger, then soy sauce and vinegar. Cook 2 more minutes.
  3. Spoon into lettuce cups and top with carrot.
Recipe 05

Salmon with Roasted Asparagus

Serves 225 minDinner

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (5 oz each)
  • 1 bunch asparagus, woody ends trimmed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, salt, and black pepper
  • 1 tsp fresh dill (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425 F (220 C). Toss the asparagus with half the oil and salt.
  2. Roast the asparagus for 8 minutes on one sheet pan. Season the salmon and place it on a second pan with lemon slices.
  3. Add both pans to the oven and bake 10 to 12 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily. Finish with dill.
Recipe 06

Black Bean & Quinoa Bowl

Serves 325 minLunch or dinner

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry quinoa, rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • Juice of 1 lime, cumin, chili powder, and cilantro

Steps

  1. Cook the quinoa in 2 cups water for 15 minutes, then rest covered for 5 minutes.
  2. Warm the beans and corn with cumin and chili powder for 4 minutes.
  3. Build bowls with quinoa, beans, avocado, salsa, lime juice, and cilantro.
Recipe 07

Zucchini Noodles with Shrimp

Serves 215 minDinner

Ingredients

  • 2 medium zucchini, spiralized
  • 8 oz raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Red pepper flakes, lemon juice, and parmesan (optional)

Steps

  1. Saute the shrimp in oil with garlic for 2 minutes per side. Remove from the pan.
  2. Add the tomatoes for 2 minutes, then the zucchini noodles for 2 minutes. Keep them al dente.
  3. Return the shrimp, add lemon juice and pepper flakes, and serve right away.
Recipe 08

Chickpea Spinach Curry

Serves 430 minDinner

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic and 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder and 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup light coconut milk

Steps

  1. Cook the onion in oil for 5 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, curry powder, and cumin for 1 minute.
  2. Stir in tomatoes and chickpeas. Simmer 12 minutes.
  3. Fold in spinach until wilted, add coconut milk, and serve with brown rice or on its own.
Recipe 09

Overnight Oats with Chia

Serves 15 min + overnightBreakfast

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 banana, sliced, or 1/2 cup berries
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon and a few drops of vanilla

Steps

  1. Stir the oats, milk, chia, cinnamon, and vanilla in a jar.
  2. Refrigerate at least 6 hours or overnight.
  3. Top with yogurt and fruit in the morning. Eat cold or warm it for 1 minute.
Recipe 10

Tofu Cauliflower Stir-Fry

Serves 325 minDinner

Ingredients

  • 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 3 cups cauliflower florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil and 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar and 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp garlic and 1 tsp ginger, minced

Steps

  1. Crisp the tofu in avocado oil over medium-high heat, 8 minutes, turning often.
  2. Add cauliflower and pepper with a splash of water. Cover and steam-fry 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in garlic, ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, and honey. Cook 1 minute more and finish with sesame oil.

Small habits, big results

Batch your protein

Cook two proteins on Sunday. Use them in salads, wraps, and bowls all week.

Fill half your plate

Start each meal with vegetables. They add volume, fiber, and color for fewer calories.

Drink water first

Have a glass of water before meals. Many people find it helps them recognize fullness.

Keep the kitchen stocked

Frozen vegetables, canned beans, and plain Greek yogurt make healthy cooking easy.
